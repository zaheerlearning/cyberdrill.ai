🎯 What is Phishing?

Phishing is a cyber attack where criminals impersonate legitimate organizations to trick you into revealing sensitive information, clicking malicious links, or downloading malware. 91% of data breaches start with a phishing email.

🚩 Top 10 Red Flags
  • Urgency & Pressure: "Act now!" "Within 24 hours!"
  • Suspicious Sender: Check email address for typos (amaz0n.com vs amazon.com)
  • Generic Greetings: "Dear Customer" instead of your actual name
  • Spelling/Grammar Errors: Professional companies proofread carefully
  • Requests for Sensitive Info: NO legit company asks for passwords via email
  • Suspicious Links: Hover over links to see the real URL destination
  • Unexpected Attachments: Especially .exe, .zip, .scr files
  • Too Good to Be True: "You've won $10,000!" or "40% guaranteed returns!"
  • Threatening Language: "Account will be closed!" "Legal action pending!"
  • Mismatched Domains: Email claims to be from PayPal but sent from paypa1-security.net
✅ Best Practices
  • Verify Before You Click: Go directly to websites by typing the URL yourself
  • Use Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA): Adds an extra security layer
  • Keep Software Updated: Security patches protect against new threats
  • Use Password Managers: Generate and store unique passwords for every account
  • Report Suspicious Emails: Forward to your IT security team immediately
  • Trust Your Instincts: If something feels wrong, it probably is
🎭 Common Attack Techniques
  • Email Phishing: Mass emails sent to thousands of targets
  • Spear Phishing: Highly targeted attacks using personal information
  • Whaling: Attacks specifically targeting executives and high-level personnel
  • Smishing: Phishing attacks via SMS text messages
  • Vishing: Phone calls from scammers impersonating legitimate organizations
  • Business Email Compromise (BEC): Attackers pose as executives requesting wire transfers
🛡️ If You Click a Phishing Link - Act Fast!
  • Don't Panic: Quick action can minimize damage
  • Disconnect from Internet: Prevent malware from spreading or communicating
  • Don't Enter Credentials: Close the browser window immediately
  • Change Passwords: Use a different, clean device to update passwords
  • Run Antivirus Scan: Check for malware installation
  • Report to IT Security: Notify your security team right away
  • Monitor Your Accounts: Watch for unusual activity or unauthorized access
  • Enable MFA Everywhere: Add extra protection layers to all accounts
📞 Important Resources
  • Federal Trade Commission: ReportFraud.ftc.gov
  • IRS Phishing Reports: phishing@irs.gov
  • Anti-Phishing Working Group: reportphishing@apwg.org
  • Check if You've Been Compromised: haveibeenpwned.com
  • Your IT Security Team: Always your first line of defense!
🎓 Remember: Cybersecurity is an ongoing journey, not a destination. Stay vigilant and keep learning!
