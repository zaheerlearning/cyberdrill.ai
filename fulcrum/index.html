OT Watch Items
Key Industrial Protocols
⚠ MODBUS / TCP
Unauthenticated by design. Shodan shows 47,000+ exposed. Critical to ensure no internet-routable access from manufacturing floor.
⚠ EtherNet/IP
Common in Allen-Bradley PLCs. CIP protocol vulnerabilities regularly disclosed. Monitor ICS-CERT for Rockwell patches.
⚠ PROFINET
Siemens-dominant protocol. Ensure PROFINET devices are in isolated VLAN. Monitor for DCP flooding attacks.
⚠ DNP3
Common in energy/utilities. Vulnerable to replay attacks. Authentication extension (DNP3 SA) should be enforced where supported.
